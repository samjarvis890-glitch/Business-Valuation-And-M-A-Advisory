STEP-BY-STEP PREPARATION

Business Valuation Preparation Checklist

Comprehensive documentation and qualitative data essential for rigorous, defensible business valuation appraisals.

01

Financial Statements & Tax

Core historical financial baseline covering operating revenue, margins, and capital structure.

  • 3 to 5 years audited or reviewed income statements
  • Year-to-date interim P&L vs prior-year comparison
  • Complete federal & state corporate tax returns
  • Balance sheets with aging schedules for AR and AP
Discuss Information Requirements
02

Normalized EBITDA & Add-backs

Adjustments necessary to determine sustainable economic cash flow unburdened by discretionary items.

  • Owner compensation & executive market-replacement delta
  • Non-operating assets, personal expenses & perks
  • One-time litigation, restructuring or moving charges
  • Facility lease normalisation vs commercial fair market rates
Request EBITDA Normalisation Review
03

Commercial & Operating Context

Strategic fundamentals that directly govern discount rate selection and risk premia.

  • Top 10 customer revenue concentration analysis
  • Key supplier agreements & long-term commercial contracts
  • Proprietary IP, patents, software licences & trademarks
  • Management organogram and key-person dependency review

ANALYTICAL RIGOUR

Financial Information Preparation Guide

How corporate leaders should structure internal accounts to withstand independent audit, buyer diligence, or board scrutiny.

1. Reconcile Accrual vs Cash Accounting

Ensure revenue recognition matches delivery obligations under ASC 606. Mismatched billings and unearned deferred revenue frequently cause last-minute purchase price adjustments during transactions.

2. Isolate Maintenance vs Growth Capex

Distinguish capital expenditures required to sustain current revenue from discretionary growth investments. This is critical for establishing true Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE).

3. Establish Target Net Working Capital (NWC)

Model normalized 12-month trailing working capital cycles to determine baseline liquidity requirements and avoid post-closing working capital disputes.

TRANSACTION PREPARATION

M&A Readiness & Diligence Framework

The four foundational pillars every company must prepare prior to initiating an acquisition, buyout, or structured sale.

PILLAR 01

Virtual Data Room (VDR) Architecture

Standardized folder taxonomy spanning corporate charter, capitalization tables, material contracts, tax returns, and IP assignments.

Readiness Timeline: 30–60 Days
PILLAR 02

Quality of Earnings (QofE) Report

Sell-side financial review identifying EBITDA adjustments, margin durability, and customer churn drivers before buyer diligence begins.

Readiness Timeline: 45–75 Days
PILLAR 03

Management Presentation & CIM

A structured Confidential Information Memorandum supported by analytical proof-points rather than ungrounded marketing rhetoric.

Readiness Timeline: 30–45 Days
PILLAR 04

Closing Mechanism & Working Capital

Clear formulation of completion accounts, earn-out mechanics, rollover equity structures, and escrow holdback arrangements.

Readiness Timeline: Pre-LOI Phase

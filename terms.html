GOVERNANCE & LEGAL FRAMEWORK

Terms & Conditions of Advisory

Understanding the professional parameters, illustrative educational boundaries, and formal mandate terms of Northbridge.

Last Updated: November 2026 • Professional Advisory Terms

1. Educational & Informational Scope

The articles, case studies, scenario analyses, financial calculators, and frameworks published on this website are presented strictly for educational, illustrative, and conceptual discussion purposes.

No content published on this website constitutes formal business valuation advice, investment recommendations, securities underwriting, or a solicitation to buy or sell corporate equity or assets.

2. Formal Advisory Mandates

A formal advisory relationship with Northbridge Valuation & Advisory is established exclusively upon execution of a bilateral, written Engagement Letter detailing the agreed scope of work, methodology, deliverable specifications, fee structure, and limitation of liability.

3. Independent Valuation Standard

Where Northbridge is commissioned to deliver an independent valuation report, opinion of value, or financial due diligence assessment, such work is conducted with objective analytical rigor in accordance with recognized professional valuation standards (e.g., IVS / APES 225).

4. Intellectual Property

All website content, proprietary frameworks, analytical diagrams, and editorial materials remain the intellectual property of Northbridge Valuation & Advisory Pty Ltd and may not be reproduced without prior written consent.

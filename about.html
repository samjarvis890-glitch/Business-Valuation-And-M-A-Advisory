IMPORTANT BUSINESS QUESTIONS

The Right Questions Bring Greater Clarity.

Business owners and decision-makers may face important financial questions at different stages of their business journey.

Business value may be influenced by financial performance, business structure, operating conditions, risks, market considerations and expectations about future performance.

Relevant information can include financial statements, management accounts, business plans, operating information and details about the organisation's business model.

Financial analysis helps decision-makers understand performance, identify trends, consider assumptions and organise information relevant to business decisions.

Transaction preparation may involve objectives, financial information, business operations, relevant risks, transaction structure and strategic considerations.

A useful starting point is to clarify objectives, organise relevant information and identify the financial and commercial questions that require attention.
